Your tabs, in OpenAudio.

Click the OpenAudio toolbar button in a playing tab to give it independent audio controls. Click again to restore ordinary playback. The mixer lives in the OpenAudio app; no popup needs to stay open.

Output device access

To reveal additional speakers and headphones, Chromium requires microphone permission. This button requests access, immediately stops the temporary microphone stream, and refreshes the available outputs. No microphone audio is saved or sent.

System default works without this additional permission.

Vinyl track details

Allow metadata access to read song titles, artists and album covers from playing tabs across websites while the Vinyl window is open. This does not capture audio. Without this grant, only tabs you have approved through the toolbar can expose details. Some pages do not publish metadata.
